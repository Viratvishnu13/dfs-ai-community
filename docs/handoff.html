  • Vision Section: Stage 02 removed completely; exclusively Stage 01 (AI Superusers → AI Builders).
  • CTA Placement: No repetitive buttons; CTAs live strictly at the end of the page (Join AI Club and Have an idea, submit here).
  • Footer: Minimal text: ©️DFS AI Club - 2026.
  • Browser Subagent: Do not spawn browser subagents; user provides visual screenshots directly.
